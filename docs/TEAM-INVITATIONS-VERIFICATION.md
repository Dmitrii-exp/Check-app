# Email invitations and roles — 1.2.6

Implemented 2026-10-20 against Dmitrii-exp/K2-trener invitation behavior.

* Network manager prepares an invitation with email and department, then sends it.
  The joining capability is delivered only by email, not returned to the manager.
  SHA-256 hashes are stored in a private, unexposed schema; raw tokens use 32 random
  bytes. Links expire after seven days, are one-use and can be replaced/revoked.
* New invitees enter name/password/repeat and get a confirmed Auth account and an
  employee profile. Existing accounts must prove their existing password. No
  account is moved between companies and invitations cannot reset passwords.
* Only a stored network-manager profile can assign any of the three roles.
  Department leads are department-bound; the last network manager is protected.
* Subscription/payment data remain manager-only. Staff receive operational limits
  without tariff names, subscription dates or prices. RLS prevents their billing
  reads, company deletion, role escalation and equipment/action writes.
* Employees can complete due maintenance/assigned work and create consumable
  requests. They cannot edit existing consumable records/photos. Due maintenance
  tasks are generated by a scoped, atomic server RPC from actual saved rules.
* Old shared-code joining is revoked. Client-wide bulk snapshot upserts were
  removed; individual mutations persist directly, reducing stale-data overwrites.
